# Fonts

**Transport Heavy** (`transport-heavy.woff`): the lettering of British road signs, designed by Jock Kinneir and Margaret Calvert. This file is converted from *AG Transport Heavy* by smugpie (https://github.com/smugpie/uk-ire-road-sign-fonts), which is derived from the Department for Transport's working drawings. Those drawings are Crown Copyright, available under the Open Government Licence v3.0: https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/

**Atkinson Hyperlegible** (`atkinson-regular.woff`, `atkinson-bold.woff`): by the Braille Institute of America, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (see `OFL-atkinson-hyperlegible.txt`).
